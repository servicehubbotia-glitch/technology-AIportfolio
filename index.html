HOW I WORK

Personalisation goes beyond a name on a screen.

It starts with what someone already knows, what they want to achieve, how they work and what gets in their way. Technology should adapt to people. These questions guide requirements, interface choices and testing.

Understand the context → define the scope → build and test → refine through use. Integration, diagnostics and clear documentation support the work throughout.

03 / SERVICEHUB

AI & Digital Literacy

Helping non-technical audiences understand AI, question its outputs and use digital tools with greater independence. Everyday situations make the questions concrete.

Course in preparation

Practical AI literacy

A learning programme currently being developed for people who want to understand and use AI without needing a technical background. It focuses on informed choices, information quality and digital autonomy.

Content and learning activities are being prepared. The course has not yet been delivered and is not currently offered for enrolment.

Questions the work addresses

  • What can AI do, and where are its limits?
  • How do we check a response, its sources and its uncertainty?
  • How can we use AI critically and independently?
  • How can a real need become a personalised digital tool?
  • What does responsible use mean in an everyday situation?

Proposed formats
