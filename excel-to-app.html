THE PROBLEM AND THE OPPORTUNITY

A spreadsheet is a starting point, not always the best interface.

Excel is valuable for tabular records, but a growing collection of activities can be difficult to explore consistently. A purpose-built interface can display relevant fields, support controlled entry and produce understandable summaries without forcing a user to navigate raw rows.

01 /

Before: spreadsheet entry

Dates, activities and results are recorded in cells. The person must interpret the layout and keep the structure consistent.

02 /

Engineering transformation

The data is modelled as records, with validation, an interface, access rules and reporting separated from the underlying spreadsheet.

03 /

After: usable information

A user can navigate records, inspect a selected item, review weekly totals and generate outputs through a dedicated application.

THE ACTUAL SOFTWARE ENGINEERING

What MyReintegration really implements.

The original application is a private activity and application journal, not a generic office document-management package. Its implementation demonstrates reusable engineering methods.

01

Structured entry

Responsive forms capture activity descriptions, dates, status, duration and results. Records can be reviewed and edited by authorized users.

02

Private data connection

Google OAuth and an Apps Script API connect the interface to a private spreadsheet with service-side access controls and revision checks.

03

Reporting & portability

Weekly views and reports support Excel, PDF, CSV, JSON and editable email drafts. Exports preserve useful structured information.

04

Recovery & consistency

Draft recovery, guarded retries, backups and conflict detection help avoid losing work when connections are interrupted.

Portfolio scope: the office dataset above is hypothetical. The real MyReintegration app solves an individual record-keeping need. Adapting this approach to another organization would require its actual document types, permissions, retention rules and workflows. No automatic Excel-to-app conversion or production document storage is claimed.
