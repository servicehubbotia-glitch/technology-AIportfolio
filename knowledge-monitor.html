A WORKING SYSTEM, NOT A MOCKUP

What the monitoring actually does.

The source collection was catalogued first. The watcher then compares trusted identifiers, revisions and fingerprints, logs results and preserves the difference between discovery and approval.

01 /

Source governance

Official institutions, scientific studies, BOTIA criteria and unclassified material remain distinguishable. Each catalogue entry can carry version, provenance, licence and SHA-256 information.

02 /

Repeatable source checks

Python adapters check verified EUR-Lex consolidations, EFSA publications, FDA/eCFR references, Codex and WHO/JECFA/IARC material.

03 /

Three explicit outcomes

SIN_CAMBIOS, VERSION_NUEVA and CONSULTA_FALLIDA are different results. A network or verification failure is never treated as an unchanged source.

04 /

Human review and safeguards

Detection events remain DETECTADO until an editorial decision. Source version records and the catalogue are not silently updated by the monitoring job.

ENGINEERING CAPABILITIES / BUSINESS APPLICATIONS

Designed for decisions, not just data collection.

The same engineering methods can support other document-heavy domains that require traceability, reproducible checks and reviewable changes.

⌘

Source provenance

Keep track of where each assertion originates and whether a document is official, a study or an internal interpretation.

↻

Scheduled change detection

Monitor selected references daily, retain historical comparisons and handle inaccessible sources explicitly.

⊞

Reproducible audit trails

Persist dated outcomes, record updates without duplicates and protect editorial data from automated alteration.
